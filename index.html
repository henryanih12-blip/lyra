Week 1 · 25 Nov 202601The baseline

Four worlds, one heartbeat

Your collection doesn't belong to one scene. It's split between 90s and 2000s R&B, Atlanta melodic trap, Nigerian Afrobeats and underground UK rap, and each one holds its own.

What links them is feeling. Thug Cry and Make It Out sit as easily next to Be Without You as Essence sits next to Real Roses. Pain turned into melody, love turned into rhythm.

The most personal corner is your UK side. Beezo, Trezz0, Lay8om and Loyal DTM aren't chart picks. You went digging for them, and that instinct is exactly what Lyra builds on.
